# StepUp — 러닝 크루 (working store name: "StepUp — 러닝 크루")

- What it is: A free Android running app for Korean runners: GPS run tracking, crews with shared "crew runs", and a game layer where you earn in-app points (SUP) by running and collect/upgrade virtual sneakers.
- Who it is for: Korean recreational runners (roughly 20s–40s) who run with friends or a running crew, or want a reason to keep running; secondarily people who liked move-to-earn apps such as STEPN.
- What it sells, at what price: Free. No in-app purchases, no paid draws, no ads, no subscription today. SUP points cannot be cashed out in the store build. **There is no revenue model yet** (open question).
- Where and how: Online, Android only (Google Play, Korea first; ko/en/ja/zh). Not yet on the store — a test APK is distributed directly. No marketing channel chosen yet.
- Budget and constraints: Built by one founder working with AI coding tools. Budget not stated. Blockchain features (wallet, token withdrawal, NFT minting on the GIWA chain) are switched off in the store build because Google Play requires a Korean virtual-asset-business registration for wallet/exchange apps since 2026-01-28; legal review of SUP/wallet/exchange and a location-service business filing are still to do.
