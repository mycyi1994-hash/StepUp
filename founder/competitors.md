## 2. Price range of paid tiers

Most competitors are free. Paid tiers are subscriptions; reward apps earn from ads or crypto instead.

- **Lowest:** Garmin Connect+ at £6.99/month or £69.99/year ([BikeRadar](https://bikeradar.com/news/garmin-connect-plus)), US$6.99/month ([itc.ua](https://itc.ua/en/news/garmin-adds-lots-of-ai-to-garmin-connect-app-with-6-99-month-subscription/)). It sits on top of a watch you have already bought.
- **Middle:** Strava at about ₩80,000/year. This is user-reported in a [2023 KR Play review](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.strava&hl=ko&gl=KR) and is no longer purchasable in Korea.
- **Highest, and the only Korean one confirmed:** Runday 올데이 패키지 at **₩9,900/month or ₩99,000/year** ([KR App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id1042937618)).
- **Outlier:** STEPN, where you have to buy an NFT sneaker before you earn anything (US$1,200+ at its 2022 peak per [ForkLog](https://forklog.com/en/investors-money-funds-stepn-how-the-move-to-earn-app-monetises-running/); current price not checked).
- **Free, funded by ads or by a parent business:** NRC, Cashwalk (ads), Samsung Health, Running Life, 러닝킹.
- **Paid prices I could not find:** in-app prices for 랭킹마라톤, 모두의 러너 and 먼데이런클럽 are not on their public listings.

## 3. Positioning map

The two axes that matter to this buyer:
- **X:** how much the app is about *running together with your crew in person* (left = solo, right = run together).
- **Y:** whether running *earns something* (bottom = no reward, top = points, cash or tokens).

```
              EARNS A REWARD
                    ^
   STEPN (tokens,   |
   pay-to-start)    |
   Cashwalk (cash,  |                         [ empty: StepUp's target —
   walking)         |                           in-person crew runs with
   빅워크 (donation) |                           shared stakes, free to start ]
                    |
   랭킹마라톤 (medals, virtual races, territory game)
                    |   모두의 러너 (team rankings)   러닝킹 (crew rankings, 0 users)
 SOLO --------------+-----------------------------------------------> TOGETHER
                    |   Runday (remote crews, friend cheering)
   Garmin Connect   |   NRC (challenges)         먼데이런클럽 (in-person matching)
   Samsung Health   |   Strava (clubs; left KR)  Instagram/Daangn/Kakao crews
   watch apps       |                            소모임/문토
                    v
               NO REWARD
```

The reward apps all sit on the solo side: Cashwalk and STEPN are about your own steps. The in-person "together" apps and channels pay nothing and track nothing shared. Everything between the two is either remote/virtual (Runday crews, 랭킹마라톤) or a leaderboard (모두의 러너, 러닝킹).

## 5. THE GAP

**No app here makes the in-person crew run itself the thing that counts and earns, without making the runner pay first.**

The evidence:
- Korean crews organise on Instagram ([KB Think](https://kbthink.com/main/living-finance/consumption-life/mz-consumption/mz-running.html)), Daangn and KakaoTalk ([Daangn posts](https://daangn.com/kr/group-post/p8wned3yutjm/)), and general meetup apps (소모임, 문토). None of these tracks the run.
- The apps with "crews" do it at a distance:
  - Runday's crews are described as "비대면으로 각 지역의 러너분들과 소통과 경쟁" (remote) ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id1042937618)).
  - 랭킹마라톤 is "비대면/언택트".
  - 모두의 러너 and 러닝킹 are leaderboards.
  - 먼데이런클럽 matches people in person but rewards nothing and tracks nothing shared.
- The apps that reward running are solo and have the strongest complaints:
  - STEPN needs money up front and users say they never earn.
  - Cashwalk earns on ads and users resent the forced live shopping.
- Strava, the main global social-running app, left Korea in March 2025 ([Asia Economy](https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025032416121224051)). That opened a space for a Korean-available social running app.
- StepUp's free shoes plus anti-cheat speak to themes 2 and 6. Its shared crew run speaks to the empty top-right of the map.

**Where the evidence stops:**
- No source shows that crews *want* a game layer on their runs. The demand evidence is for running together and for rewards *separately*.
- Cashwalk's users stay for real money ("모은 캐시로 직접 필요한 물건도 구매할 수 있어서 더 좋네요" — [Cashwalk](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cashwalk.cashwalk&hl=ko&gl=KR)). StepUp's store-build points have no cash value, so its reward is weaker than Cashwalk's.
- The gap is real but unproven. Treat it as a hypothesis to test with one or two actual crews, not as a demonstrated demand.

## 6. THE THREAT

**Runday (TTAM) could copy StepUp fastest.**
- It is Korean and already has 100만+ Android installs, a ₩9,900/month subscription, **crews, friends with live cheering, and shoe management** in the product ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id1042937618), [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hanbit.rundayfree&hl=ko&gl=KR)).
- Adding a synchronized "start together" crew run, plus shoe-wear and points on top of its existing shoe tracker, is a feature release for them, not a new product. They could fund it from the subscription StepUp doesn't have.

**Second: Cashwalk.**
- It has 1,000만+ installs and a working ad-funded points economy that pays real goods.
- A "run with your crew, earn more cash" mode would beat StepUp's no-cash-value points on motivation the day it ships.

**NRC and Garmin are less likely to copy.** Both are global and slow to build Korea-specific features. Garmin is moving features behind Connect+.
