A running app worth opening without the token

Most M2E apps died when emissions fell, because the token was the product. Delete SUP from StepUp and it is still a running app people use.

Real GPS courses

Record a run, save the track as a course, publish it. Other runners take it and run it. Completion pays km × 1.0 SUP, capped at 42 — deterministic, no loot box.

Speed-verified runs

GPS segments faster than 25 km/h are discarded, and a session that is mostly implausible is voided outright. A car ride does not become a run.

Rankings that reward different runners

Top speed, time on feet, and total SUP are three separate boards — plus a faction ranking where every kilometre counts for the sneaker you wore.

Capital cannot outrun legs

The strongest possible sneaker earns 17.8% more per step than a free one. Not 3×, not 10×. That ceiling is a constant in the contract, not a parameter someone can raise.

Built for GIWA

M2E settlement is a high-frequency, low-value workload: many small claims, every day, from consumer phones. It needs cheap, predictable execution on a chain where a consumer app is a first-class citizen.

SUPToken

ERC-20, 1B hard cap, no mint function after deploy. Supply can only fall.

SneakerNFT

ERC-721 with stats on-chain, so a sneaker's earning power is verifiable rather than asserted.

RewardDistributor

Signed run proof, replay-protected, charged against a halving daily budget. No owner withdrawal path.

CourseRegistry

Course authorship is public and permissionless; the reward formula is a pure function.
